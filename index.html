OUR PHILOSOPHY

Growing with the child — step by step.

Discover the educational philosophy behind Almita and our child-centred approach to development, relationships and learning.

Almita brings a German educational perspective shaped by a holistic and child-centred understanding of development. Children are not expected to develop everything at once. Each stage of childhood has its own needs, rhythms and ways of learning.

0–7 years — Learning through experience

In the early years, children discover the world through imitation, movement, sensory experiences, emotional bonds, creativity and free play. A safe relationship and a predictable daily rhythm create the foundation for confidence and healthy development.

7–14 years — Learning through connection

As children grow, thinking, emotions and social awareness develop further. Creativity, imagination, friendships, responsibility, empathy and belonging become increasingly important. Almita aims to support this development through meaningful activities, movement, relationships and age-appropriate guidance.

Our principle:
Safety → Trust → Belonging → Development → Learning

BAHRAIN PARENT SURVEY 2027

Help us design care around real family needs.

Almita is exploring a flexible, safety-centred concept for Bahraini and international families: Early Years Care for children aged 0–4 years and After-School Care for children aged 4–12 years, until parents finish work or are able to collect them. This survey is for market research only and does not represent an already licensed service.

Anonymous • approx. 3 minutes • optional contact.

PROPOSED SERVICE MODEL

Early Years Care • 0–4 years: Morning Care approx. 7:30–13:00 and Full Day Care approx. 7:30–18:00.

Pre-Booked Care on Selected Days • 0–4 years: Care reserved in advance for specific selected days.

After-School Care • 4–12 years: School dismissal–18:00: homework, movement, board games and creativity.

Languages: Age-appropriate English • Arabic • German • Spanish.

Transport: Optional potential school pick-up / transport, subject to approval and demand.
