WHY ALMITA

Childcare should work with family life — not against it.

Almita is currently in market-validation and regulatory-planning stage. The concept aims to support working families with flexible care while keeping safety, emotional wellbeing, movement, play and respectful relationships at the centre.

The family challenge

Parents may need dependable care not only while they work, but also for appointments, changing schedules and the hours between school dismissal and the end of the working day.

The Almita response

A welcoming daily-care environment for approximately ages 0–4, combined with a separate after-school offer for primary children: homework support, movement, creative play, board games and optional language experiences.

Respect for Bahrain

A concept designed to listen first, respect local culture and family values, and serve both Bahraini and international families.

PROPOSED SERVICES

FOUNDER & EDUCATIONAL DIRECTOR

Alvaro Marcelo Prieto Ypanaque

Almita is shaped by 13 years of professional experience with children, young people and families across education and social work, supported by German academic and professional qualifications. The founder’s background includes residential child and youth welfare, child protection, safeguarding, de-escalation and non-violent communication.

His life and work across Peru, Germany and Vietnam bring an international perspective. Spanish heritage and a German educational and professional background support a family-centred vision that aims to listen first and respect Bahrain’s culture and family values.
